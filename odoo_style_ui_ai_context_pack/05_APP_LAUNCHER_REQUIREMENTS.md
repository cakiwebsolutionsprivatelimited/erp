# App Launcher Requirements

## Purpose
The app launcher is the heart of the Odoo-style modular product.

## Required App States
- Installed
- Available
- Locked
- Upgrade Required
- Coming Soon

## Apps to Show
CRM, Sales, Billing, Inventory, Purchase, Accounts, HRMS, Payroll, Projects, Helpdesk, Field Service, Marketing, Website, Reports, Studio.

## App Card Fields
icon, name, category, short description, status badge, action button, plan availability, quick stats if installed.

## Filters
All, Sales, Finance, Inventory, HRMS, Services, Marketing, Website, Productivity.

## Actions
Open installed app, install available app in demo mode, show upgrade modal for locked apps, show coming soon modal for coming soon apps.
