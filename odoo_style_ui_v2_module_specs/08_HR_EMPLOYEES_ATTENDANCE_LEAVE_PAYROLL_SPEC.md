# HR, Employees, Attendance, Leave and Payroll Module Detailed UI Requirement

## Purpose
Manage employees, attendance, leave, salary, payroll, documents, and HR reports.

## Sidebar
Dashboard, Employees, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Departments, Documents, Reports, HR Settings.

## HR Dashboard
Widgets:
Total employees, Present today, Absent today, On leave, Pending leave requests, Payroll due, New joiners.

Charts:
Attendance trend, Department headcount, Leave summary, Payroll cost trend.

## Employees
Columns:
Employee ID, Name, Department, Designation, Phone, Email, Joining date, Status.

Employee Form sections:
Personal details, Contact details, Job details, Salary details, Bank details placeholder, Documents placeholder.

Employee Detail Tabs:
Overview, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Documents, Notes.

## Attendance
Views:
Daily attendance, Monthly attendance, Employee-wise attendance.

Fields:
Employee, Date, Check-in, Check-out, Status, Work hours, Location placeholder.

Statuses:
Present, Absent, Half Day, Late, Leave, Holiday.

## Leave
Columns:
Employee, Leave type, From date, To date, Days, Reason, Status.

Statuses:
Pending, Approved, Rejected, Cancelled.

## Payroll
Screens:
Salary structures, Payroll processing, Salary slips, Advance salary, Loan placeholder, Reimbursement placeholder.

Fields:
Employee, Basic salary, Allowances, Deductions, PF, ESI, Net salary, Payment status.

## Reports
Attendance summary, Leave report, Salary register, Overtime report placeholder, Compliance report placeholder.

## Demo Functionality
Add employee, mark attendance, apply leave, approve/reject leave, generate salary slip demo.
