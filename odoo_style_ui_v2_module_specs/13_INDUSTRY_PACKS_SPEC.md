# Industry Packs Detailed UI Requirement

## Purpose
Industry Packs combine existing modules into ready-made solutions for specific business types.

## Apps
Pharmacy, Gym, Clinic, Distributor, Service Company.

## Common Industry Pack Page
Each pack should show:
Industry overview, Included modules, Recommended workflows, Key dashboards, Setup checklist, Demo data, CTA Request Setup/Install Demo/Upgrade.

## Pharmacy Pack
Included:
Billing, Inventory, Purchase, Products, Expiry tracking placeholder, CRM, Reports.

Screens:
Pharmacy dashboard, Medicine inventory, Batch/expiry list, Sales billing placeholder, Purchase list, Low stock and expiry alerts.

## Clinic Pack
Included:
Appointments, Patients, Billing, CRM, Reports.

Screens:
Clinic dashboard, Appointment calendar, Patient list, Doctor schedule, Prescription placeholder, Billing placeholder.

## Gym Pack
Included:
Members, Subscriptions, Attendance, Trainer assignment, Workout media placeholder, Payments.

Screens:
Gym dashboard, Members list, Membership plans, Renewals, Attendance, Trainer schedule, Workout library placeholder.

## Distributor Pack
Included:
Sales, Billing, Inventory, Purchase, Warehouse, CRM.

Screens:
Distributor dashboard, Dealer/customer list, Orders, Stock, Purchase, Outstanding payments.

## Service Company Pack
Included:
CRM, Projects, Helpdesk, Field Service, Billing.

Screens:
Service dashboard, Service requests, Technician schedule, Work orders, Customer tickets, Payment collection placeholder.

## Demo Functionality
Show pack dashboard, show included modules, install demo pack locally, create demo records placeholder.
