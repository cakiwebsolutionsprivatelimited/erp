# Sales, Quotations and Subscriptions Module Detailed UI Requirement

## Purpose
Manage quotations, proposals, sales orders, recurring subscriptions, and sales performance.

## Sidebar
Dashboard, Quotations, Sales Orders, Products/Services, Customers, Subscriptions, Reports, Sales Settings.

## Sales Dashboard
Widgets:
Total quotations, Pending quotations, Accepted quotations, Sales orders, Monthly sales value, Recurring revenue, Expiring subscriptions.

Charts:
Quotation conversion trend, Sales by user, Sales by product/service, Monthly recurring revenue.

## Quotations List
Columns:
Quotation number, Customer, Date, Expiry date, Salesperson, Amount, Tax, Total, Status, Actions.

Statuses:
Draft, Sent, Viewed, Accepted, Rejected, Expired, Converted to Order.

Actions:
Create quotation, Send quotation demo, Preview PDF, Duplicate, Convert to sales order, Mark accepted/rejected.

## Quotation Form
Sections:
Customer details, Quotation items, Taxes/discount, Terms and conditions, Notes, Attachments placeholder.

Fields:
Customer, Quotation date, Expiry date, Salesperson, Product/service, Quantity, Unit price, Discount, GST rate, Line total, Subtotal, Tax amount, Grand total, Terms, Notes.

## Sales Order
Columns:
Order number, Customer, Quotation reference, Order date, Delivery date, Amount, Status.

Statuses:
Confirmed, Processing, Delivered, Cancelled.

## Products/Services
Fields:
Name, Type Product/Service, SKU optional, Category, Unit, Price, GST rate, Description, Status.

## Subscriptions
Columns:
Subscription number, Customer, Plan/service, Start date, Renewal date, Billing cycle, Amount, Status.

Statuses:
Trial, Active, Renewal Due, Expired, Cancelled.

Detail tabs:
Overview, Billing schedule, Invoices placeholder, Customer notes, Activity timeline.

## Reports
Quotation conversion, Sales order summary, Subscription renewal, Salesperson performance, Product/service sales.

## Demo Functionality
Create/edit quotation, add items, calculate GST total locally, change quotation status, preview quotation layout, convert quotation to sales order, create subscription demo.
