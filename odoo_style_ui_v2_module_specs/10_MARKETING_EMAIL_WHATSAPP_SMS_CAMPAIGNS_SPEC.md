# Marketing, Email, WhatsApp, SMS and Campaigns Module Detailed UI Requirement

## Purpose
Manage campaigns through email, WhatsApp, SMS, and lead nurturing automation placeholders.

## Sidebar
Dashboard, Campaigns, Email, WhatsApp, SMS, Audience, Templates, Reports, Marketing Settings.

## Dashboard
Widgets:
Total campaigns, Active campaigns, Messages sent, Open rate placeholder, Click rate placeholder, Leads generated, WhatsApp credits, SMS credits.

Charts:
Campaign performance, Channel-wise messages, Lead generation trend.

## Campaigns
Columns:
Campaign name, Channel, Audience, Status, Scheduled date, Sent, Leads generated, Actions.

Statuses:
Draft, Scheduled, Running, Completed, Paused, Failed.

## Email Campaign
Fields:
Campaign name, Subject, Sender, Audience, Email template, Schedule, Preview.

## WhatsApp Campaign
Fields:
Campaign name, Template, Audience, Variables, Schedule, Credit estimate.

## SMS Campaign
Fields:
Campaign name, Message, Audience, Schedule, Credit estimate.

## Audience
Segments:
All leads, Customers, Lost leads, Follow-up due, Industry-specific, Custom segment.

## Templates
Fields:
Template name, Channel, Content, Variables, Status.

## Reports
Campaign report, Channel performance, Audience engagement, Lead conversion.

## Demo Functionality
Create campaign demo, schedule campaign locally, preview templates, view reports demo.
